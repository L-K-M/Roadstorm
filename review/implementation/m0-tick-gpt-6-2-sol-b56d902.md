## What the snapshot supports

The saved fixed, default and max pacing runs report 128 contract, 76 N1 and 108 structural checks passing per mode. The removed-settling mutation reports seven failures, including measured undercounts of 11 on the second 12-tick request and zero on the final one-tick request. The frozen-wait checks sample a dynamic body across 30 engine frames. Completion ordering is observable: a pausable node before the coordinator receives 12 callbacks, while one after it receives 11. Authoritative simulation work therefore belongs inside the coordinator, with the coordinator owning tree pause.
- **Reset, endings and movement.** Reset clears probe accounting, pending results, RNG and history, restores the probe pose and freezes. Finish and cap checks follow each committed logical tick, so early endings report elapsed ticks and subsequent terminal requests return zero. The warmed over-envelope positive-request test checks for zero integrations. Motion uses a nonmutating `cast_motion` and diagnostic `test_move` before one explicit `move_and_collide` per segment. The saved traces exercise one car and a wall. Full participant reset and shared interactive invocation remain open integration work.
- **Throughput and cost.** The retained comparison reports 600 ticks in **0.170 s accelerated** and **10.881 s paced**, with matching final position and per-tick trace digests. Fifty settling iterations cost roughly **0.833 s** at 60 Hz, giving a **0.923×** ideal paced ceiling. The result measures this one-car fixture. Bridge, multi-car and training throughput remain unmeasured.
