## Ranked implementation findings

1. **Medium, blocking — The sampler silently exceeds its arc-length tolerance on a regular short span.** `src/course/road_arc_sampler.gd:787–812` accepts the first Simpson refinement on its Richardson estimate alone. The pinned-engine probe used cubic controls `(0,0,0), (1/3,-3.17/3,0), (2/3,(5-6.34)/3,0), (1,1.83,0)`. This is the planar quadratic `B(t)=(t,5t²−3.17t,0)`, with speed `sqrt(1+(10t−3.17)²)` and no zero derivative. Its closed-form length is 3.1086978943 m. The sampler succeeded at 1 m and 4 m spacing, returning 3.0815344493 m with an estimated error of 0.0008250634 m under the default 0.001 m tolerance. Actual error was 0.0271634450 m. At 1 m spacing the first interior sample's analytic station was 0.999997 m, so this probe isolates the length error from the interior inversion. Use a stronger acceptance check for quadrature and add an analytic cancellation regression for the total and near-end spacing. The existing independent reference in `tools/road_sampling_lab/road_sampling_checks.gd:394–465` covers one different curve.
2. **Medium, blocking — The hairpin fixture guard accepts missing required source values.** `tools/calibration_hairpin_path_lab/calibration_hairpin_path_source.gd:303–320,342–355` uses NaN as the fallback and then compares the absolute difference with a tolerance. NaN fails the greater-than test, so the converter reports success after deleting the fixture arc's `radius_m` or the primary run's `initial_state.velocity_m_s`. This bypasses the fixture agreement and speed-provenance guard described in `README.md:35–38,146–147`. Require numeric, finite values before tolerance comparisons, including analogous bay and gate checks. Add missing-scalar regressions through `build()`.
3. **Medium — A positive span can collapse to a zero-width global station interval.** `src/course/road_arc_sampler.gd:392–429,1452–1463` checks local length and global finiteness but never requires the sum to advance on each span. The pinned probe successfully parsed an X span ending at X=2^53 followed by a Z span from 0 to 0.25 m at that X. With spacing 1e16 m, the sampler succeeded with two endpoint samples and reported the second span's local length as 0.25 m while its global `start_s == end_s`. Global station lookup cannot represent that span's interior. Refuse the whole table when a positive span fails to advance the station and cover this with a large-prefix regression.
4. **Low — The hairpin filter accepts inconsistent ordered section/surface tags.** `tools/calibration_hairpin_path_lab/calibration_hairpin_path_source.gd:385–419,445–469,570–572` checks section membership and forwards the sample tags but does not validate each tag against its owning interval and section surface. The pinned probe changed raw sample 1 from approach/asphalt to exit/gravel and the filter still succeeded. The frozen builder's actual stream has the expected tags. Check ownership and surface against the ordered record, with explicit allowance for the two section-side copies at joins.
