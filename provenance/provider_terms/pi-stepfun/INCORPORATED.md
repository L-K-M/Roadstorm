The [open-platform user agreement](user-agreement.20261126T214653Z.html)
§1.1.6 says generated content is for reference and should not be used for
commercial purposes. Its §5.2 places output IP with the user or legal rights
holder where law allows, while §2.2.4 requires accurate AI-generated labels
when content is distributed. The incorporated management rules separately
prohibit removing, altering or hiding generation labels. The privacy policy
was captured because the user agreement incorporates it; this review found no
broader output-use permission there.

**Disposition: unresolved rights gate.** Output ownership does not itself
remove the commercial-use language or labeling condition. Do not treat
StepFun-authored output as cleared for unrestricted Unlicense/CC0 release
without resolving those conditions and the actual Step Plan document's
applicability. This is a reading of the published text, not a legal opinion.
